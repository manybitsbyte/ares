The whole disc is held in memory, and briefly twice over while it loads — once as the track files the sheet names and once as the image built from them. A 578 MB game peaked at about 1.6 GB of tab here, and took under three seconds to reach the logo. No disc byte is ever written to browser storage: neither the BIOS nor the game leaves this page, the memory cards persist only through the buttons above, and the one thing kept between visits is a pointer to the folder you chose — tens of bytes that name it, not a copy of anything in it.
